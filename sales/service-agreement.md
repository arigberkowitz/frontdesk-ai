## The honest fine print

**It's AI.** It handles the large majority of calls well, and it will
occasionally misunderstand someone. That's why every call is recorded and every
miss becomes a fix. It is not for emergency calls (911-type situations) and it
doesn't give medical, legal, or financial advice.

**Recording laws.** The system plays a recording disclosure to callers. Some
states require this; keeping the disclosure on, and confirming it satisfies
your state's rules, is your responsibility. We default it to on.

**Texting your customers.** Reminder and follow-up texts to your customers only
go to people who called you first, and anyone can stop them by replying STOP.
You agree not to use the service for cold texting or spam.

**Your data.** Your call data belongs to you. We process it through the vendors
that run the service (voice, AI, texting, email) and never sell it. Full detail:
[your-domain]/privacy.

**If things go wrong.** Our total liability is capped at what you paid us in
the last 12 months. We're not liable for the value of a missed call or booking
— telephone networks and third-party services fail in ways nobody can fully
control, which is also why you keep a forwarding number.
