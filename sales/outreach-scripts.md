# Outreach scripts — first customers

Rules that make these work: reference something real from THEIR business
(hours, a service, a review), one clear ask, no AI hype words. Say "one flat
monthly price". Never put a demo or test phone number in outreach, and don't
pitch medical or health businesses.

What a self-serve trial really gets (keep claims to this):
- 14 days free, no card. The AI is drafted from their website and works in
  the browser right away.
- Its own phone number as soon as they make one test call in the browser.
  They forward their business line to it when they're ready.
- Booking only after they connect a calendar (Google directly; Outlook, Apple
  and others through a free Cal.com account). Before that, it takes a
  message instead.
- Owner alerts by email (and text, with text alerts on) when it books someone
  or takes a message. There is no text after every call; every call's
  recording and summary are in the dashboard. The `/growth`
page drafts a personalized version of the cold email for any prospect site —
these are the hand-tuned templates behind it.

## The pitch meeting (15 minutes)

1. **Open with their pain, not the product** (2 min): "How many calls do you
   miss a day? What happens when someone calls at 8pm?"
2. **Live demo on THEIR business** (5 min): onboard their website in front of
   them, make the browser test call (that gives the trial its own number),
   then call that number on speaker. Booking needs their calendar connected
   (Settings → Calendar); otherwise show it taking a message. Then show the
   call transcript + summary landing in the dashboard.
3. **Show the compounding part** (3 min): the portal's "Your AI learned"
   panel and prompt version history — it gets smarter from their real calls,
   with their approval on every change.
4. **Price + close** (5 min): "One flat monthly price: Missed-Call Rescue
   $199, Starter $399, or Pro $599. Starter is less than one missed job.
   14 days free, no card; you're only charged if you pick a plan. Want me
   to point your number at it today?"

## Objection cheat sheet

- **"My customers hate robots."** — It answers naturally, in 10 languages,
  and offers a human handoff for anything sensitive. Play them a real call.
- **"I already have voicemail."** — Voicemail doesn't book appointments.
  ~⅓ of voicemail callers never call back.
- **"What if it says something wrong?"** — It only answers from the
  knowledge you approve; every call is recorded and QA-graded; and it never
  invents prices or hours. Anything it wants to learn, you approve first.
- **"Too expensive."** — What's your average job worth? One saved
  after-hours call a month covers it.
