# Outreach scripts — first customers

Rules that make these work: reference something real from THEIR business
(hours, a service, a review), one clear ask, no AI hype words. The `/growth`
page drafts a personalized version of the cold email for any prospect site —
these are the hand-tuned templates behind it.

---

moment someone leaves a message. English and Spanish. It costs less per month

Fastest way to judge it: I'll set one up from your website in about 10
minutes and you can call it yourself. Free to try for 14 days. Worth a look?

## The pitch meeting (15 minutes)

1. **Open with their pain, not the product** (2 min): "How many calls do you
   miss a day? What happens when someone calls at 8pm?"
2. **Live demo on THEIR business** (5 min): onboard their website in front of
   them, call the number on speaker, book an appointment, then show the call
   transcript + summary landing in the dashboard.
3. **Show the compounding part** (3 min): the portal's "Your AI learned"
   panel and prompt version history — it gets smarter from their real calls,
   with their approval on every change.
4. **Price + close** (5 min): "$300/mo Starter, less than one missed job.
   14-day free trial, no charge until you go live. Want me to point your
   number at it today?"

## Objection cheat sheet

- **"My customers hate robots."** — It answers naturally, speaks Spanish,
  and offers a human handoff for anything sensitive. Play them a real call.
- **"I already have voicemail."** — Voicemail doesn't book appointments.
  ~⅓ of voicemail callers never call back.
- **"What if it says something wrong?"** — It only answers from the
  knowledge you approve; every call is recorded and QA-graded; and it never
  invents prices or hours. Anything it wants to learn, you approve first.
- **"Too expensive."** — What's your average job worth? One saved
  after-hours call a month covers it.
